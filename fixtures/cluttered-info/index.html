Council Tax Reduction

Council Tax Reduction (sometimes called CTR) can lower your council tax bill if you are on a low income or claim certain benefits. The amount you get depends on your income, your savings, who lives with you and your circumstances.

Important: you must apply within 1 month of moving into the borough or of your income changing, otherwise your reduction may only be backdated from the date we receive your application.

Who can apply

  • People who pay council tax for the home they live in
  • People on a low income, including pensioners and people in work
  • People who receive Universal Credit, Pension Credit, or Jobseeker's Allowance

You usually cannot get a reduction if you have savings over £16,000 (unless you receive the guarantee part of Pension Credit).

What you need before you start

DocumentExample
Proof of incomeLast 2 payslips or pension statement
Proof of savingsBank statements from the last month
Your council tax account numberOn your bill, 8 digits starting with 6

How to apply

Applying takes about 20 minutes. You can save your progress and come back later.

After you apply

We aim to process applications within 14 working days. We will write to you to tell you the decision and your new bill. If you disagree with the decision you can ask us to look at it again within one month.

Data protection: we use the information you give us to assess your claim and may share it with the Department for Work and Pensions to prevent and detect fraud. Giving false information is a criminal offence and may result in prosecution under the Council Tax Reduction Schemes (Detection of Fraud and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2013.
